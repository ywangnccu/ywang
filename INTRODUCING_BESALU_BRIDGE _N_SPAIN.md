Besalu Bridge in Spain is approximately 105 meters long and forms the historic entrance to the Besalu town. It is a magnificent example of architecture. 
This iconic stone bridge features a distinctive angular or slightly bent layout because its piers were built on rocks. The Besalu Bridge has a fortified design with multiple arches.
It includes a prominent fortified gateway tower that historically served as a defensive fortification. In 1939, few of its arches were blown up, severely damaging the bridge.
It was subsequently reconstructed during the 1950s and early 1960s. The bridge is an important part of Besalu's landscape. It offers panoramic views of the town's stone buildings,
the riverbanks and nearby historical sites and structures. Once you cross it, you enter the narrow streets and enjoy exploring the excellent architecture of the historic town. 
This Bridge is particularly striking when viewed from the approach to the Besalu town. It is one of the most recognizable symbols of Catalonia.
