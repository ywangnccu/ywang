The Blue Bridge in Michigan is a cool functional bridge around the downtown Grand Rapids area linking very busy high traffic areas to each other. It's an amazing addition to the downtown area. The bridge was originally constructed as a railroad bridge. 
Trains crossing this bridge traveled between Muskegon and Mackinaw City. Now the Blue Bridge is an iconic landmark to the city and is one of the longest truss bridges in Michigan. It's fun to visit at any season of the year. 
Often you'll see people fishing off the bridge or taking images for special occasions. Sometimes fitness classes take place on the Blue Bridge and often you'll see a horse-drawn carriage.
